# Submission checklist

- [ ] Verify the author, affiliation, funding, conflict-of-interest, corresponding-author, and CRediT statements already populated in `manuscript/manuscript_expanded.md` and `manuscript/cover_letter_expanded.md` (not placeholders) are current for this submission round.
- [ ] Confirm the sponsoring institution's ethics determination for the secondary analysis of de-identified public data.
- [ ] Confirm journal scope and current author instructions before upload.
- [ ] Review the manuscript, supplement, figures, and cover letter as a complete submission.
- [ ] Confirm Zotero import and apply the journal's required citation style if it differs from AMA.
