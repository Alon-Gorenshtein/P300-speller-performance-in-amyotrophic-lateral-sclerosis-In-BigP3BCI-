# TRIPOD+AI checklist for prediction model development and evaluation

Reporting was informed by the TRIPOD+AI statement (Collins GS, Moons KGM, Dhiman P, et al.
Transparent reporting of a multivariable prediction model for individual prognosis or diagnosis
statement: TRIPOD+AI. *BMJ*. 2024;385:e078378. doi:10.1136/bmj-2023-078378), which extends the
original 2015 TRIPOD statement to prediction-model studies that use regression or machine-learning
methods and, per its own text, replaces TRIPOD-2015. This checklist is completed against the
submitted manuscript. This study is not a conventional diagnostic or prognostic clinical prediction
model: it evaluates a previously proposed calibration-to-accuracy relationship in an existing
archive rather than developing a new model or predicting a clinical outcome, so items specific to
model development are marked not applicable and the reason is given, following TRIPOD+AI's own
Development (D) / Evaluation (E) applicability distinction. Section and table references below
follow the manuscript's current headings; where a checklist item is only partly addressed in the
text, that gap is stated rather than papered over.
