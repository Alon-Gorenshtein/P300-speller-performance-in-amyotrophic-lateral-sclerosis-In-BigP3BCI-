# Citation audit

Six references were checked before manuscript rendering.

- References 2 through 6 were verified by DOI against Crossref. Author lists, titles, venues, years, and article locations were compared with the verification report.
- Reference 1 is a dataset record whose DOI is not registered in Crossref. The verifier's title-search fallback was rejected because it returned an unrelated conference paper. The citation was manually replaced from the official PhysioNet version 1.0.0 record, which lists the five authors, publication date, version, and DOI.
- The online-first metadata for Ryan et al. was reconciled to the 2018 issue year shown in PubMed, rather than Crossref's 2017 online-publication year.

The generated files are `references_source_AMA.txt`, `references_source_IEEE.tex`, and `references_source_for_zotero.bib`. The final AMA bibliography is the source of truth for the manuscript reference list.
